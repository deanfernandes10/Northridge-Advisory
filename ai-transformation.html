Digital Employee Experience

DEX: make workplace technology work better for employees.

Digital Employee Experience (DEX) looks at how employees experience devices, applications and IT support. It combines technical performance data with employee feedback to identify friction and guide improvements.

I built and managed DEX offers at NWN and DXC. NorthRidge brings that offer-management experience to Digital Workplace strategy and technology decisions.

Understand the experience

Assess device and application reliability, employee feedback, adoption and support demand. Agree which experience measures matter for your workforce.

Prioritize practical improvements

Connect findings with proactive support, appropriate automation and a clear improvement roadmap. Evaluate AI capabilities alongside security, privacy and employee needs.
