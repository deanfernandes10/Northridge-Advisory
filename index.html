Common Questions

Flexible ways to work with NorthRidge.

Can I hire Dean for a full-time executive role?

Yes. Dean is open to full-time technology executive opportunities. Contact him to discuss the organization, leadership mandate and long-term priorities.

How does the partner ecosystem support an engagement?

Dean helps identify where specialist expertise or delivery capacity would support your objectives. Partner scope, responsibilities and working arrangements are agreed for the engagement, with a shared direction alongside your internal team.

What is the difference between fractional, part-time and interim leadership?

Fractional leadership owns an agreed executive mandate for a portion of time. Part-time leadership usually provides ongoing responsibility for a broader function on a regular schedule. Interim leadership fills a temporary need, often at greater intensity, during a transition or permanent search. The scope and decision rights matter more than the title.

Can NorthRidge work alongside our existing executive?

Yes. A Transformation Accelerator gives one major initiative dedicated executive ownership. Your existing leader retains responsibility for the long-term function, with decision rights and collaboration agreed at the start.

How long does an engagement last?

Duration depends on the mandate. The 90-Day Executive Accelerator establishes the diagnosis, agenda and initial execution. Advisory and fractional retainers can continue as needed; interim and project assignments follow an agreed transition or completion plan.

Is the work remote or on-site?

NorthRidge offers remote-first executive leadership with on-site participation for planning sessions, workshops and key milestones where agreed. Cadence and travel are discussed when scoping the engagement.

When is a permanent executive the better choice?

A permanent role is often appropriate when the business needs continuous daily leadership of a large function, enduring ownership of culture and talent, or a permanent scope that warrants a full-time seat. Fractional work also needs sponsorship, authority, access and resources to succeed.

How are scope and fees determined?

Engagements are scoped around the outcomes, intensity, duration, executive access and responsibility required. A conversation about your hurdle establishes whether a retainer, interim assignment or fixed-scope assessment is the appropriate starting point.

How do we measure progress and avoid dependency?

Agree the baseline, success measures, owners and milestones at the start. Review delivery and business outcomes regularly. Coaching, documented decisions, repeatable governance and an explicit handover help your team continue the work.
