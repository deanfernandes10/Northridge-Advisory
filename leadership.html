Common questions

When is a permanent executive the better choice?

When the organization needs daily leadership of a large function, enduring ownership of culture and talent, or a permanent scope that warrants a full-time seat. Dean also welcomes full-time executive opportunities.

How long does an engagement last?

Duration follows the mandate. An initial 90-day agenda can establish the baseline, align priorities and begin execution. Fractional work may continue on an agreed cadence; interim and project assignments include a transition or completion plan.

Is the work remote or on-site?

Remote and on-site participation are agreed around the business need, including planning sessions, workshops and key milestones.

How are scope and fees determined?

Scope and fees reflect the outcomes, intensity, duration, access and responsibilities involved. The first conversation establishes whether a retainer, interim assignment or defined assessment is appropriate.

Can specialist partners support the work?

Yes. Where additional capabilities are useful, specialist scope, responsibilities and working arrangements are agreed for the engagement. Explore the ecosystem.
